RULES & FAQ
⚠️ You MUST reveal after round ends▼
After the round timer hits zero, you have 1 hour to submit your reveal. Miss this window and your bet is permanently forfeited — added to the next round's seed pool. No exceptions, no refunds.
💸 Seed at risk if round fails▼
Create a custom round and zero players reveal? Round is cancelled and your seed is refunded. If even one player reveals, round finalizes and your 10% creator fee is paid from the pool.
🔑 Lost your browser? Recover your guess▼
Your salt is deterministic — derived from your wallet address. Clear browser, switch device — doesn't matter. During reveal phase hit "Can't find your guess? Recover it", enter your original guess, contract verifies on-chain instantly.
🎯 How prizes are split▼
Of the 80% player pool — 55% to 1st · 20% to 2nd · 10% to 3rd · 15% split by bet size across all revealers. Tie broken by earliest commit block. Remaining 20% = 8% protocol + 12% seeds the next round.
🔒 Is the number truly random?▼
Yes — 11 entropy sources mixed: prevrandao, timestamp, block number, prev block hash, base fee, sequencer, round ID, global nonce, pool amount, category seed, creator address. Target hash published before any bet is accepted — cannot be changed.
🏗️ Can I create my own round?▼
Yes — go to the Create tab. Deposit a minimum seed of 0.0005 ETH, pick category and duration. You earn 10% of the total pool when the round finalizes. Your seed is always refundable if the round is cancelled.
